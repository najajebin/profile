I study how the brain keeps time and how to read the signals it produces. So far that has meant fly genetics at the bench and machine learning on EEG data.

I’m a third-year student majoring in Biology with a minor in Data Science. Over the past year I have worked on the circadian control of egg-laying in Drosophila, classified EEG signals with machine learning, and tested botanical alternatives to chemical pest control. I want to combine experimental design with the analysis of the data it produces, and I’m looking for a lab where I can contribute to ongoing work and learn from it.
